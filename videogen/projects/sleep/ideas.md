# Ideas: why we need sleep

Pick one: copy its block (from its ## line to the next) into idea.md in this folder, edit it
as you like, then run: videogen/make_video.sh --project sleep

## 1. The Brain's Night Shift

**Hook:** Did you know your brain works when you sleep?

**Central picture:** A night shift worker in a brain-shaped office

**Outline:**
1. Your brain doesn't shut down when you sleep.
2. It's actually on a night shift, cleaning up your thoughts.
3. It sorts out memories and clears out mental clutter.
4. This is why you feel groggy if you wake up in the middle of the night.
5. Sleep is your brain’s way of doing a mental deep clean.
6. Rest is how your brain resets for the next day.

_Why it works: This angle makes sleep feel like a job with purpose, which is relatable and surprising._

## 2. The Secret Garden of the Mind

**Hook:** What if your brain had a secret garden?

**Central picture:** A garden with flowers growing from brain cells

**Outline:**
1. Imagine your brain is a garden.
2. During sleep, it waters the flowers of your memories.
3. It prunes the dead thoughts and lets the good ones bloom.
4. Without sleep, the garden gets overgrown and chaotic.
5. This is how your mind stays balanced and creative.
6. Sleep is the gardener, not a luxury.

_Why it works: This metaphor is vivid and relatable, using a familiar image to explain complex brain processes._

## 3. The Missing Link in Your Day

**Hook:** You’re missing something essential every day.

**Central picture:** A puzzle missing one piece with a sleep symbol in the gap

**Outline:**
1. Every day, you’re missing a key part of your routine.
2. That missing piece is sleep, not just rest.
3. It’s the link between your thoughts, body, and emotions.
4. Without it, your mind can’t process the day properly.
5. It’s like trying to run a race without your legs.
6. Sleep isn’t optional — it’s the missing link that keeps you going.

_Why it works: This angle turns sleep into a critical missing piece of the puzzle, making it feel urgent and essential._

## 4. The Dreamer’s Secret Weapon

**Hook:** Your dreams are helping you solve real problems.

**Central picture:** A person with a brain that’s a locked box with a key labeled 'sleep'

**Outline:**
1. Your dreams are not just random images.
2. They’re your brain solving puzzles you can’t solve while awake.
3. It’s like your subconscious is working on your problems in the dark.
4. This is why you wake up with new ideas or solutions.
5. Dreams are your brain’s way of thinking without limits.
6. Sleep is the key to unlocking your creative power.

_Why it works: This angle taps into the mystery of dreams and makes sleep feel like a superpower, which is highly engaging._

## 5. The Body’s Invisible Repair Crew

**Hook:** Your body is fixing itself while you sleep.

**Central picture:** A repair crew working on a car with sleep symbols on their helmets

**Outline:**
1. While you sleep, your body is doing invisible repairs.
2. It’s fixing your muscles, your immune system, and your brain.
3. This is why you feel better after a good night’s sleep.
4. Without sleep, your body can’t catch up on damage from the day.
5. Sleep is your body’s way of maintaining itself.
6. Rest isn’t just for your mind — it’s for your whole body.

_Why it works: This angle shifts focus from the mind to the body, offering a fresh perspective that’s both practical and powerful._
