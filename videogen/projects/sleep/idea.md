## 1. The Brain's Night Shift

**Hook:** Did you know your brain works when you sleep?

**Central picture:** A night shift worker in a brain-shaped office

**Outline:**
1. Your brain doesn't shut down when you sleep.
2. It's actually on a night shift, cleaning up your thoughts.
3. It sorts out memories and clears out mental clutter.
4. This is why you feel groggy if you wake up in the middle of the night.
5. Sleep is your brain’s way of doing a mental deep clean.
6. Rest is how your brain resets for the next day.

_Why it works: This angle makes sleep feel like a job with purpose, which is relatable and surprising._

**Trusted fact to use:** during deep sleep, fluid flows through the brain and washes away waste that builds up while you are awake (scientists call this the glymphatic system).
**Tone:** calm, curious, friendly. End by telling the viewer to protect their sleep tonight.
